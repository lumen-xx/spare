# Spare 0.5.0

- **Privacy & Debloat**: four new pages, Apple Intelligence, Data Sharing, Apple Apps and Annoyances, with switches for what macOS does. You review every change before it's made. Settings only a profile can keep off go into one configuration profile, “Spare Privacy”, which a short guide walks you through installing and which Spare can remove again. Apple's built-in apps can't be deleted, so Spare keeps them from opening and takes them out of the Dock. Nothing under /System changes and System Integrity Protection stays on.
- **Undo and Uninstall** in Settings: tick what to put back from everything Spare changed, then, if you like, move Spare and its files to the Trash. [docs/UNDO.md](https://github.com/lumen-xx/spare/blob/main/docs/UNDO.md) shows how to undo each setting by hand, too.
- **Finder settings** in Settings: path bar, status bar with the space left, full paths in the title, hidden files, filename extensions, folders on top, the Library folder, how new folders open, and more.
- **Appearance**: Automatic, Light or Dark. And you can stop Spare asking before it moves things to the Trash.
- **Privacy permissions of programs known only by their path** (applets, scripts, tools) can be deleted now, as can those of apps with spaces in their identifier. When something can't be deleted, the message has a Copy Error button.
- Right-click any file, app or leftover for Show in Finder and Copy Path. Rows on Leftovers, an app's details and Purgeable Space are selected by clicking anywhere on them.
- A cleaner sidebar: the main pages on top, then titled sections with a thin line along their pages. Sections fold away and stay that way; the list fades out at both ends, and the disk sits across the bottom.
- The Apps page points Spare's own row to Uninstall in Settings.
