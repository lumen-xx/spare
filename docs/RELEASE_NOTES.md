# Spare 0.4.0

**New pages**
- **Purgeable Space** shows what macOS counts as System Data and gives back by itself, part by part, as macOS's own clean-up service reports it. Free Now asks macOS to remove the files apps marked purgeable and downloads nothing uses any more. Time Machine's local snapshots, which had their own page, are listed here.
- **macOS Downloads** is rebuilt. One Free button moves what an old update left in "macOS Install Data" to the Trash, where History can put it back, and has macOS remove the downloads nothing needs. Apple Intelligence can be switched off to free its models, after a sheet that says exactly what changes, and switched on again. Every other download says what keeps it.
- **Cloud Downloads** lists files iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Google Drive and Dropbox keep downloaded while they're also online. Removing a download works like Finder's Remove Download: the file stays in the cloud.
- **Simulators** shows Xcode's simulators and simulator systems. The ones that can't run any more can be deleted together.
- **Docker** shows the disk file of Docker Desktop, OrbStack, Colima or Podman. Build cache, unused images and stopped containers are cleared with Docker's own commands.
- **Browser Extensions** lists extensions in every profile of Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox and Safari, and marks the ones that can read every website.

**Cleanup**
- Finds the caches Chromium browsers and Electron apps such as Slack and VS Code keep beside their data, and the copies Mail saved of attachments you opened.
- Your Call lists Messages attachments of 5 MB or more. They're never selected.

**A new look**
- Warm, frosted paper with the desktop showing through, hairline cards and springy motion, with a small drawing when a page has nothing to show. With Reduce Motion everything crossfades; with Reduce Transparency the paper is solid; with Increase Contrast the lines are stronger and the paper grain goes.
- Set in Bricolage Grotesque, with its optical sizes: open at caption size, tight for the big numbers.
- When something doesn't work, Spare says what happened and offers the next step, such as Try Again.
- All coloured text meets WCAG contrast in light and dark mode.
- The disk card in the sidebar shows free space, then the Trash.
